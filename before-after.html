Healthyfy Hosting Move

Our apps used to run in many separate places. Now almost everything runs in one Kubernetes setup on staging, with fewer databases and a smaller bill. The live app will get a copy of the same setup at launch.

Before

flowchart LR
  users([App, coaches, team])
  subgraph AKS["Kubernetes, 1 small server"]
    api1[API]
    portal1[Coach portal]
  end
  subgraph AS["App Service, 3 plans"]
    strapi1[Strapi]
    lf1[Langfuse]
    bot1[Old chatbot]
  end
  subgraph VM["Virtual machine"]
    mat1[Matomo]
  end
  subgraph CA["Container Apps"]
    mem1[Team memory]
    cont1[Content tools]
  end
  subgraph DB["5 database servers"]
    my57[(MySQL 5.7)]
    pg1[(Postgres Strapi)]
    pg2[(Postgres Langfuse)]
    pg3[(Postgres memory)]
    redis[(Redis, unused)]
  end
  users --> api1 & portal1 & strapi1 & lf1 & mat1 & mem1 & cont1
  api1 --> my57
  mat1 --> my57
  strapi1 --> pg1
  lf1 --> pg2
  mem1 --> pg3
  lf1 -.-> redis

Secrets lived inside the cluster, there were two image registries, and testing and the live app shared one database server.

After

flowchart LR
  users([App, coaches, team])
  gh[GitHub Actions] -- deploys with no downtime --> ing
  subgraph AKS["Kubernetes, 2 to 4 servers, grows when busy"]
    ing{{One front door with HTTPS}}
    subgraph STG["staging"]
      api2[API x2 + worker]
      portal2[Coach portal x2]
      strapi2[Strapi x2]
      lf2[Langfuse]
      mat2[Matomo]
    end
    subgraph TOOLS["tools"]
      mem2[Team memory]
    end
  end
  kv[[Key Vault: all secrets]]
  subgraph DB["2 database servers"]
    my80[(MySQL 8)]
    pgs[(Shared Postgres)]
  end
  users --> ing
  ing --> api2 & portal2 & strapi2 & lf2 & mat2 & mem2
  kv -.-> AKS
  api2 --> my80
  mat2 --> my80
  strapi2 --> pgs
  lf2 --> pgs
  mem2 --> pgs
  users --> cont2[Content tools, unchanged]

Azure Functions, Firestore and Cosmos DB are unchanged. The live app gets its own copy of this setup at launch.
